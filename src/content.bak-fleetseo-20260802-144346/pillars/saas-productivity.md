## What belongs in “productivity SaaS”

| Category | Examples | Overlap trap |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Docs / wiki | Notion, Coda, Confluence | Two wikis for the same team |
| Design | Canva Pro, Adobe CC, Figma | Canva + Adobe for one social channel |
| Office | Microsoft 365, Google Workspace | Paying personal + employer copy |
| Storage | Dropbox, iCloud+, OneDrive | Same files synced three places |
| AI add-ons | Notion AI, Canva Magic | Stacked on top of [AI tool subs](/pillars/ai-tools) |

## Seat and family math

**Per-seat billing** explodes when you add a spouse “just in case.” Rules:

- **One paid seat** per household for creative tools unless both publish weekly.
- Use **guest / viewer** free tiers for collaborators.
- Employer-provided M365 or Google → cancel personal duplicate immediately.

Family plans (Apple, Google, Microsoft) belong in [family subscriptions](/pillars/family-subscriptions) — do not double-buy individual SaaS on the same Apple ID.

## Annual vs monthly decision

Use [annualize math](/pillars/annualize-math):

```
annual_cost = monthly_price × 12
effective_monthly = annual_prepay ÷ 12
```

Prepay **only** if:

1. You used the tool **weekly** for the last 90 days, and  
2. Cancel path is documented before renewal (see [cancel playbook](/pillars/cancel-playbook)).

Otherwise stay monthly — productivity churn is high; annual traps fund unused features.

## Cut list (SubAudit standard)

Cancel or downgrade when **both** are true:

- No export, publish, or client deliverable in **45 days**.
- Another tool in your stack already covers 80% of the workflow.

## VPN and security overlap

Do not count **VPN** here — it’s a privacy category with different renewal psychology. See [VPN privacy guide](/pillars/vpn-privacy).
