## When family plans win

| Plan type | Typical win | Break-even hint |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Spotify / YouTube Family** | 2+ adults in one household | &gt;2× individual price |
| **Apple One** | Already paying iCloud + Music + TV+ | Bundle vs à la carte [annualize](/pillars/annualize-math) |
| **Microsoft 365 Family** | 6 seats, small business side projects | vs two personal $99 plans |
| **Netflix extra member** | One remote parent | Cheaper than second full sub |

## Household vs “friends and family”

Terms usually require **same address** or **same country**. Violations risk account lock — not worth $4/mo savings.

## Amazon Household

Shares **Prime shipping** and some digital benefits — **not** a free Audible account for everyone. Map each [Amazon digital](/pillars/amazon-digital-stack) line to a **named user**.

## Kids and parental controls

- Separate profiles for minors on video apps.
- Turn off **in-app purchase** passwords on shared iPads.
- Review [Subscribe & Save](/pillars/subscribe-and-save) — kids’ snacks on auto-ship add up silently.

## Splitting costs fairly

1. One card pays; others Venmo **annualized share ÷ 12** monthly.
2. Reconcile when someone adds a [Prime Video channel](/pillars/streaming-stack) without group OK.

## Audit trigger

Run family audit when:

- A member leaves for college (profile still on premium tier).
- Divorce / move-out (payment method still shared).
- Trial upgraded to **family** without telling the payer.
