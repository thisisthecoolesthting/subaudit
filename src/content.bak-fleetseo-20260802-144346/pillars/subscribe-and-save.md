## Subscribe & Save is a recurring charge

S&S hits your card on a **schedule**, not when you “feel low.” Treat it like streaming:

| Field | What to log |
|-------|-------------|
| SKU / ASIN | From order history |
| Cadence | 1–6 months |
| Unit price last ship | Compare to one-off “Buy now” |
| Pantry days on hand | Target 14–30 for consumables |

## Five-item / 15% tier discipline

Amazon’s **5+ subscriptions** tier unlocks extra discount — but five **wrong** SKUs is hoarding, not savings.

Rules:

1. Only S&S for **predictable burn** (filters, coffee, diapers).
2. Never double-subscribe the same category (two paper towel ASINs).
3. Re-run [annualize math](/pillars/annualize-math) quarterly — unit prices drift.

## Overlap with [Amazon digital](/pillars/amazon-digital-stack)

Prime membership and S&S ship discounts stack differently than **Audible** or **Prime Video channels**. Keep digital in the digital bucket; S&S in **household consumables**.

## Cancel path (physical)

1. Amazon → **Account** → **Subscribe & Save items**
2. Skip shipment **or** cancel subscription per SKU
3. Screenshot next ship date after change

Pair with [cancel playbook](/pillars/cancel-playbook) for third-party meal kits that mimic S&S psychology.

## When one-off wins

- Intro coupons on competing retailers
- Bulk warehouse club if you already pay membership
- Price increased **twice** without notice — common on consumables
