Morgan Hale is the editor of SubAudit. She spent six years in FP&amp;A at a mid-size SaaS company before a single credit-card export showed twelve overlapping subscriptions she had forgotten to cancel — three streaming add-ons, two AI tools, and four Amazon auto-ships quietly stacking on the same card.

SubAudit is her attempt to read every receipt line out loud. Every guide annualizes the monthly fee as monthly &times; 12 unless a documented annual plan is cheaper, names the trial renewal date and cancel URL, and ends with a specific swap — not a vague &ldquo;consider cutting back.&rdquo;

Morgan tracks streaming stacks, AI and productivity SaaS, Amazon digital memberships, Subscribe &amp; Save auto-ships, and the free trials that turn into quiet recurring charges. She re-checks list prices quarterly on featured stacks and answers reader corrections within 72 hours with dated amendment notes.

SubAudit takes no sponsored placements from the services it covers. Partner links use disclosed Amazon Associates and SaaS affiliate relationships; commissions never change ranking criteria or what we recommend. The full methodology is on the <a href="/methodology/">methodology page</a>.
