## What we cannot monetize directly

- **ChatGPT Plus / Team** — OpenAI has no public consumer affiliate program.
- **Claude Pro** — no blogger affiliate; enterprise partner network only.
- **Cursor** — limited referral credits for some accounts, not a site-wide program.

We still publish TCO tables because readers search for them; we monetize **adjacent SaaS** (hosting, email, SEO, writing tools) with disclosed programs.

## Example monthly stack (illustrative — check live pricing)

| Tool | Typical consumer tier | Annualized |
|------|----------------------|------------|
| ChatGPT Plus | ~$20/mo | ~$240/yr |
| Claude Pro | ~$20/mo | ~$240/yr |
| Cursor Pro | ~$20/mo | ~$240/yr |
| GitHub Copilot | ~$10/mo | ~$120/yr |

**Overlap trap:** two general chat subs + one IDE assistant often duplicates capability. Pick **one chat** + **one coding surface** unless you bill clients for both.
