## The one number that matters

Divide **annual membership cost** by **hours you actually finish**. If that exceeds what you pay per hour for entertainment elsewhere, downgrade or cancel.

## Quick comparison table

| Path | Best for | Watch for |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Library (Libby) | $0, wait lists | Not every title |
| Single ebook purchase | One-off reads | No subscription creep |
| Audible credits | 1+ finished books/month | Unused credits expiring |
| Audible Plus catalog | Podcast-style listening | Smaller selection than credits |

## Trial fine print

Before any trial: note **renewal date**, **plan tier**, and whether **credits roll**. Set a calendar reminder **two days before** renewal.
