Audible credits vs Audible Plus catalog: stop paying for the wrong tier

If you’ve ever signed up for **Audible** and felt a bit lost about the difference between Audible credits and the Audible Plus catalog, you’re not alone. Many listeners spend more than they need to by choosing the wrong Audible tier without fully understanding what each offers.

## Key takeaways

- Audible credits allow you to buy premium titles that you own forever, while Audible Plus gives you access to a rotating selection of included titles with no credits needed.  
- The Audible Plus catalog is great for casual listeners who enjoy a wide variety of genres but are okay with a smaller, curated library.  
- Audible credits are best if you want to own new releases, exclusive audiobooks, or specific titles not available in the Plus catalog.
- Knowing which tier fits your listening habits can save you money and prevent paying for features you don’t use.
- Annual membership options and trial periods can help you test which tier suits your budget and preferences.

## Understanding Audible Credits and the Audible Plus Catalog

I used to be drawn in by Audible’s promises of endless entertainment, but after auditing my own subscriptions, I realized I was paying for credits that I never used. Here’s the breakdown I learned.

### What Are Audible Credits?

Audible credits act like tokens you can exchange for most premium audiobooks in the Audible store, including hardcover bestsellers and exclusive releases. Each credit typically buys one audiobook regardless of its list price, which means you can pick anything from a $10 book to a $50 title for the same cost.

Once you use a credit on a book, that audiobook is yours — you retain access to it forever, even if you cancel your membership. This model appeals to serious listeners who have specific must-have titles on their wishlist.

### What Is the Audible Plus Catalog?

Audible Plus works more like a streaming service. Instead of credits, you get unlimited access to a curated selection of audiobooks, podcasts, and Audible Originals for a flat monthly fee. It’s a rotating library, so new titles get added or removed regularly.

The catch? Titles from the Plus catalog are only available as long as you maintain your subscription. You don’t own any of them outright. This tier is attractive if you:

- Listen to lots of audiobooks casually and don’t mind exploring new or less-expensive titles.  
- Enjoy Audible Originals and popular podcasts that often appear in the Plus catalog.  
- Prefer to avoid buying specific audiobooks outright.

## Comparing Audible Plus vs Audible Premium Plus

When I compared Audible Plus with Premium Plus (the tier that includes credits), here’s what stood out:

## Which Audible Tier Should You Choose?

If you’re wondering whether to stick with Audible Plus or upgrade to a Credits-based Premium Plus plan, consider your listening habits. I’ve helped many people like indie hackers and cord-cutters audit their subscriptions, and this checklist has proven useful:

- Do you want to own and keep select audiobooks forever?  
  - If yes, Audible Premium Plus is the way to go.  
- Do you prefer a rotating library of titles and Originals that refresh monthly?  
  - Audible Plus may suit you better.  
- How often do you finish audiobooks in a month?  
  - One audiobook or less: consider purchasing audiobooks à la carte or going Premium with 1 credit.  
  - Multiple audiobooks: mixing Audible Plus with supplemental credits could save money.  
- Do you use Audible Originals and podcasts regularly?  
  - Both tiers include Originals, but Audible Plus might be more budget-friendly.  
- Is price a major factor right now?  
  - Audible Plus’s lower cost might be sufficient depending on your habits.

## Auditing Your Audible Subscription: What I Tested

In my former life as an FP&A analyst, I’d audit expenses line by line. I applied that rigor to my Audible subscriptions to discover:

- I was paying monthly for Premium Plus but mainly used just the Audible Plus catalog titles, meaning I often left credits unused.  
- Switching to Audible Plus saved me about $6/month with no impact on my casual, exploratory listening.  
- Occasionally buying key new releases outside the subscription when a title caught my eye made more sense than automatic monthly credits.  
- Annual plans sometimes provide a discount but require commitment; I test both monthly and annual options during trial periods to find the right fit.

## How to Stop Paying for the Wrong Audible Tier

To avoid the trap of paying for credits you don't use or missing out on owned audiobooks you want, follow these steps:

1. Review your listening history for the last 3-6 months and see how many premium titles you actually owned versus streamed.
2. Evaluate whether you prefer owning audiobooks or trying a broader rotating library.
3. Compare your current monthly fee against the Audible Plus and Premium Plus plans available to you.
4. Consider using the free trial periods Audible offers to test both tiers.
5. If you keep credits unused, consider downgrading to Plus or buying books à la carte.
6. For a hybrid approach, choose Premium Plus with fewer monthly credits and supplement with Plus catalog listening.

## FAQ

### What happens to my audiobooks if I cancel Audible Plus or Premium Plus?

If you’re on **Audible Premium Plus**, audiobooks purchased with credits are yours to keep forever. If you cancel, you lose access to the subscription catalog but keep bought titles. For **Audible Plus**, you only have access while your subscription is active.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade between Audible Plus and Premium Plus anytime?

Yes, Audible lets you switch plans whenever you want. This flexibility lets you try one tier for a while and switch based on your changing listening habits.

### Is the Audible Plus catalog smaller than the full Audible library?

Yes, the Plus catalog is a curated subset designed for unlimited streaming. The full Audible library is much larger and includes all premium audiobooks you can purchase with credits.

### Are Audible credits transferable between accounts?

No, Audible credits are tied to the account they’re purchased on and cannot be transferred or shared.

### Can I buy Audible audiobooks without a subscription?

Yes, you can purchase individual audiobooks without any subscription, but subscriptions typically offer better value if you listen regularly.
