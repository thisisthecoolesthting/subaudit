## Where creep hides

1. **Your Memberships & Subscriptions** — digital trials and Prime Video channels.
2. **Subscribe & Save** — physical auto-ship with its own cadence.
3. **Audible** — separate login, same card.
4. **Amazon Kids+** — family plans easy to forget after a promo.

## 15-minute audit

1. Open Amazon → Account → **Memberships & Subscriptions**.
2. Screenshot each active line with **next billing date**.
3. Open **Subscribe & Save** → sort by next delivery.
4. Annualize: monthly × 12 + annual plans as listed.

## Cut rule

If you have not used the benefit in **45 days** and cancellation is self-serve, cancel before the next billing window closes (often 24–48h before ship for S&S).

Physical Subscribe & Save orders can still earn **product commission** via Associates when readers shop through our links; digital memberships use **bounty** links where applicable.
