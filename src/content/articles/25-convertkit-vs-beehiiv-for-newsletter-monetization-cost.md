ConvertKit vs Beehiiv for newsletter monetization cost is a common comparison for creators looking to turn their audience into income. Both platforms simplify email marketing and monetization, but their pricing models and features can lead to very different expenses depending on your subscriber count and goals.

## Key takeaways

- **ConvertKit** offers a straightforward tiered pricing model geared to creators who want seamless integrations and flexible monetization without complex fee structures.
- **Beehiiv** features a lower base price and a revenue-sharing plan that can be attractive to smaller newsletters but may become costly at scale.
- ConvertKit includes advanced automation tools and landing pages in mid-tier plans, while Beehiiv excels in built-in referral programs and native subscription monetization.
- Your choice between ConvertKit and Beehiiv for newsletter monetization cost hinges on subscriber numbers, growth expectations, and how much you value built-in monetization features versus advanced email marketing workflows.
- Evaluate beyond pricing: deliverability, ease of use, and integration support matter greatly when scaling your newsletter business.

---

## Understanding the Pricing Models: ConvertKit vs Beehiiv

I spent weeks analyzing both ConvertKit and Beehiiv plans to gauge how costs can stack up for an evolving newsletter. ConvertKit uses a flat monthly fee based on subscriber count tiers with no revenue share on paid subscriptions you set up. Beehiiv, on the other hand, offers a lower monthly starting price but takes a percentage cut of your revenue from paid subscriptions, which is crucial to consider when revenue scales.

### ConvertKit Pricing Highlights

- For up to 300 subscribers, the Creator plan starts around $29/month.
- At 1,000 subscribers, pricing rises but stays predictable with no surprise fees.
- The Creator Pro plan adds detailed reporting and advanced automations but costs more.
- No commission on paid newsletters or digital product sales processed through ConvertKit Commerce.
- You get free migration and onboarding when switching from other providers.

### Beehiiv Pricing Highlights

- Free plan available for up to 1,000 subscribers with basic features and limited emails.
- Paid plans start low (about $25/month) for 1,000 subscribers.
- Beehiiv charges a 5% revenue share on paid newsletter subscriptions on their Starter plan, decreasing with higher plans.
- Referral programs and monetization tools are native and easy to set up.
- Some advanced features, like white-labeling or large team access, require premium plans.

This cost structure means Beehiiv is potentially cheaper upfront when starting but could become pricier in the long run if you monetize heavily.

## When ConvertKit Makes More Sense for Monetization

Given my background in financial analysis, I recommend ConvertKit if:

- You expect steady, predictable expenses as your subscribers grow without unexpected fees.
- You want advanced automation workflows to segment and nurture your list efficiently.
- You plan to bundle newsletter monetization with online courses or digital product sales.
- You prioritize deliverability and want seamless integrations with e-commerce and membership tools.
- You dislike sharing revenue and prefer a transparent flat fee to forecast your costs.

ConvertKit’s Commerce features work well for creators selling subscriptions or one-time products directly via email without needing complex setup or revenue cuts.

---

## When Beehiiv is the Better Choice

Beehiiv stands out when:

- You are launching a new newsletter with under 1,000 subscribers and want low upfront costs.
- You want built-in paid newsletter subscription management and a native referral program.
- You prefer a simple interface with onboarding designed for writers and indie creators.
- You expect to grow but want to test monetization without committing large monthly fees upfront.
- You appreciate community-building tools and straightforward analytics in one interface.

For smaller newsletters testing waters or those with irregular newsletter income, the revenue share model can reduce initial outlay but requires close tracking as income grows.

## FAQ

### What is the most cost-effective platform for new newsletter creators monetizing under 1,000 subscribers?

For newsletters under 1,000 subscribers, Beehiiv’s free plan plus revenue share can offer the lowest upfront cost. However, if you expect fast growth or want more automation features upfront, ConvertKit may provide better long-term value.

### Does ConvertKit charge fees on paid newsletter subscriptions?

No, ConvertKit does not take a revenue share. You pay a flat monthly fee based on subscribers regardless of how much you earn through paid newsletters or product sales via ConvertKit Commerce.

### How much is the revenue share on Beehiiv’s paid newsletters?

Beehiiv typically takes a 5% revenue share on paid newsletter subscriptions for their Starter plan, decreasing as you upgrade to higher plans. This cut is in addition to the monthly base subscription fee.

### Which platform offers better email automation for monetization?

ConvertKit offers more advanced email automation flows, sequences, and tagging features. These are valuable for segmenting your audience and driving conversions through personalized campaigns.

### Are there differences in deliverability between ConvertKit and Beehiiv?

ConvertKit has a strong reputation for industry-leading deliverability, a key factor when relying on email for revenue. Beehiiv is competitive but newer, so your mileage may vary depending on your list hygiene and engagement.

Choosing between ConvertKit and Beehiiv for newsletter monetization cost boils down to where you are in your creator journey and which trade-offs make the most financial sense. ConvertKit is a solid pick for established creators seeking reliable, flat-rate pricing and advanced marketing tools. Beehiiv could be ideal if you want to start small with paid subscriptions and built-in referral programs, accepting revenue share fees as the cost of testing and growth.

From my time as an FP&A analyst turned subscription watchdog, I encourage careful budgeting and tracking of all recurring and variable fees when growing your newsletter business. Cost structures matter — but so does how effectively your platform helps you monetize and connect with your audience.
