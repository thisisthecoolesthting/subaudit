## Buckets that are not interchangeable

| Bucket | Paid examples | Pick at most |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **General chat** | ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Gemini Advanced | **One** |
| **IDE / code agent** | Cursor Pro, Copilot, Codeium | **One** per editor |
| **Image / video** | Midjourney, Runway, Leonardo | **One** if you ship creatives weekly |
| **API metered** | OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter | Budget cap in dashboard |

Paying for **two general chat** subs is almost always waste — same prompts, different logos.

## When a “bundle” is real

- **GitHub Copilot** bundled with student / org plans — do not add Cursor Pro on the same repo unless you measured velocity gain.
- **ChatGPT** with browsing vs **Claude** with projects — keep the one that holds your **system prompts and files**.

## Free tiers and trials

Trials roll to paid silently on the same card as Netflix. Tag them in your [subscription audit](/pillars/subscription-audit) export with `AI-TRIAL` and set cancel **before** day 7 if you only needed one project.
