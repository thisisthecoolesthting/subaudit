## Bounty vs product commission

| Model | Pays when | Best for SubAudit pages |
|-------|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Bounty event** | Free trial or paid digital signup | Audible trials, KU, Prime Video channels |
| **Product** | Qualifying physical purchase | Roku, Fire TV, routers |
| **Digital Kindle edition** | Often **0%** product | Use bounty or editorial only |

Rates change — verify in **Associates Central → Bounty Events** before publishing.

## High-signal bounties (verify live rates)

| Program | Editorial angle |
|---------|-----------------|
| Audible Standard trial | “Worth it if ≥1 book/month” + [cancel steps](/pillars/cancel-playbook) |
| Audible Premium Plus annual | Compare credits used vs [annualize](/pillars/annualize-math) |
| Kindle Unlimited trial | Library + Libby overlap |
| Prime Video paid channel | Seasonal sports — cancel after finale |

Deep service breakdown: [Amazon digital stack](/pillars/amazon-digital-stack).

## Required on every bounty link

1. **Price after trial** and billing cadence.
2. Link to [cancel playbook](/pillars/cancel-playbook) on same page.
3. Note we may earn a bounty — does not change sort order (cut rule is usage-based).

## What we avoid

- Bounty links on pages with **no** subscription substance.
- Pushing trials the author would not take after a real [subscription audit](/pillars/subscription-audit).
- Kindle **subscription edition** product links expecting commission.
