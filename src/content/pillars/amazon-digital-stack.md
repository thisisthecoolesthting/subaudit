## What counts as “Amazon digital”

These are **memberships billed by Amazon**, not Subscribe & Save physical goods:

- **Prime** (shipping + Prime Video base catalog)
- **Prime Video channels** (Paramount+, Max, sports passes — billed through Amazon)
- **Audible** (credits or Plus catalog)
- **Kindle Unlimited**
- **Amazon Music Unlimited**
- **Kids+**

Digital Kindle **subscription editions** pay **0%** product commission on Associates — use **Bounty Events** (trials and paid signups) where eligible.

## Bounty-first picks (verify rates in Associates Central)

| Service | Typical bounty angle | Editorial use |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| Audible Standard trial | Highest single digital bounty | “Worth it if you finish ≥1 book/month” |
| Audible Premium Plus annual | Paid annual path | Compare vs credits you actually use |
| Kindle Unlimited trial | Lower bounty, high overlap with library | Libby + KU math |
| Prime Video paid channel | Add-on comparison posts | Seasonal sports / one-show subs |

We only place bounty links on pages that include **cancel steps** and **renewal price after trial**.

## Overlap warnings

- **Audible + KU** — different products, but both compete for reading time; many households need only one.
- **Prime Video channel + standalone app** — you may pay twice for the same network if you forget to cancel the non-Amazon app.
- **Music Unlimited + Prime Music benefit** — Prime includes a limited catalog; Unlimited is for full on-demand.
